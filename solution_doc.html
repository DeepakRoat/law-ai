1 Problem Statement & Selection Rationale

Over 70% of common grievances in India revolve around everyday civil disputes: unreturned rental security deposits (tenancy), withheld wages / full-and-final settlements (unpaid salary), and defective online deliveries without refunds (consumer protection). While individuals suffer distinct economic damages (typically ₹15,000–₹1,00,000), hiring a human advocate is economically prohibitive and procedure-heavy.

Why this problem: Ordinary citizens lack the legal vocabulary to assert their statutory rights, draft formal notices, or counter counterparty excuses. Standard LLMs hallucinate non-existent sections, cite wrong acts, or attempt to offer dangerous pseudo-legal advice in criminal matters. NyayaMitra resolves this through a deterministic statutory grounding database, a LangGraph state machine, and a zero-tolerance citation guardrail.

2 Core Assumptions
  • Messy Multi-lingual Inputs: Users state problems in raw colloquial phrasing (Hinglish, Hindi, regional English) without legal terms.
  • Statutory Ground Truth: Advice must strictly anchor on codified Acts (Consumer Protection Act 2019, Model Tenancy Act, Payment of Wages Act 1936 / Industrial Disputes Act).
  • Zero Criminal Jurisdiction: Any police FIR, arrest, or complex criminal/title matter is outside AI safety boundaries and triggers a strict human lawyer referral.
3 Scope Boundaries
  • Supported Domains (3): Consumer Refunds, Landlord Security Deposits, Unpaid Wages/F&F.
  • Hard Out-of-Scope Escapes: IPC/BNS criminal offences, bail, cheque bounce criminal trials, property title suits, and matrimonial litigation.
  • Role Limitation: Acts as an information and document preparation aid under Advocates Act, 1961 guidelines, not formal representation.

1. Structured Intake Engine: Uses Gemini 2.5 Flash with response_schema=CaseExtraction to convert unstructured stories into chronology, facts, and available proof.
2. Dual-Layer Retrieval: Injects deterministic statutory provisions from data/statutes_kb.json supplemented by live API hits to Indian Kanoon (api.kanoon.dev).
3. Deterministic Guardrail: Regex engine intercepts all Section/Order citations. Any statute not present in the verified KB is blocked with [BLOCKED CITATION].
5 Multi-Mode Workflow Implementations
A. Explain & Draft Notice (Standard Action): Generates a 4-part legal aid document: (1) Fact Summary, (2) Grounded Rights in user's language, (3) Formal 15-Day Registered Post Legal Notice draft, and (4) Practice Arguing offer.
B. Opponent Simulator & Coach: Roleplays stubborn Indian counterparties (e.g. "₹45k deducted for repainting") and immediately provides coached 2-sentence legal shut-down counters.

6 Scaling to Every Show / Production Domain ("How It Scales")

In production scenarios (e.g., nationwide legal awareness broadcasts, televised consumer grievance helplines, high-volume grievance portals), NyayaMitra is architected for zero-downtime extensibility:

  • Schema-Driven Knowledge Ingestion: Adding new civil legal domains (e.g., RERA real estate delay compensations, Motor Vehicle Act claims, cyber phishing refunds) requires zero pipeline code changes. Adding a new entry into data/statutes_kb.json automatically registers allowed sections, search queries, and remedy steps.
  • Multi-Backend Grounding API: Modular adapter pattern in statutes_loader.py supports official Indian Kanoon token auth, open REST endpoints, or enterprise internal case-law vectors.
  • Stateless Client Caching: Gemini client singleton caching (with v1beta HTTP options) minimizes handshake overhead, scaling easily on serverless container orchestrators (Render, Cloud Run, AWS ECS).
  • Localized Multi-Lingual Adaptation: Prompt architecture decouples language style detection from statutory reasoning, allowing the system to scale to 12+ Indian regional dialects without retraining.

8 Known Failure Modes & Defensive Mitigations

1. Scanty / Ambiguous User Information:
Failure: Users sometimes type 1 line ("Company fired me"). Hallucinating details is dangerous.
Mitigation: CLARIFY mode activates if critical facts are missing, asking at most 1–2 polite clarifying questions before drafting.

2. Criminal / High-Stakes Manipulation:
Failure: Users asking for anticipatory bail or criminal defense strategies.
Mitigation: Strict classifier redirects to escalate_to_lawyer, blocking all legal section hallucination and providing government legal aid contact numbers (NALSA Helpline 15100).

3. Statutory Section Hallucinations:
Failure: LLMs frequently invent non-existent sub-clauses.
Mitigation: Regex guardrail cross-checks every cited section token against data/statutes_kb.json. Any mismatch is redacted.

4. External Legal API Timeouts:
Failure: Indian Kanoon server latency or rate limits.
Mitigation: Graceful fallback pipeline: if the live API is slow or offline, NyayaMitra falls back safely to verified local statutory grounding without halting execution.
